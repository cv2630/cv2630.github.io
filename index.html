🌌 Hello! I'm Vincent Chen. 🌌

I'm a current undergraduate at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, studying Mathematics and Computer Science.

Previously, I was a student at Stuyvesant High School in New York, New York. There, I was the president of the ACS Chemistry Club, Computer Science Dojo, Alumni Mentoring Program, and Math Survey.

In addition, I was also a Management & Operations Intern at the NYC Department of Parks and Recreation, where I oversaw various volunteer projects and funding allocation throughout Manhattan's 200+ parks. I also conducted independent research through the American Chemical Society, working as a Research Intern at Rutgers University Newark on carbon nanotube membranes and their applications in hydrogen transport.

In my free time, I enjoy blogging, music curation, and long walks.
